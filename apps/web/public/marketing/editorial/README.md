# Flights and Umrah imagery

Created on 9 November 2026 for the local flights and Umrah redesign.

## Original service photography

Downloaded from the public STS Umrah website at the user's request. These
photos were converted to WebP without generative changes, retouching, or
resizing. The gallery uses crops; its full-size viewer preserves each original
composition. They represent the source site's service collection, not an
inventory guarantee for an individual package.

## Generated destination artwork

The two hero assets were created with the built-in image generation tool and
converted to quality-88 WebP. Both are captioned as destination artwork in
the interface. They are not photographs of an STS service or evidence of a
specific booking. Existing real group photography remains in `/about/`.

- `flights-dubai-dawn.webp`: a new cinematic Dubai coastline panorama.
- `umrah-madinah-twilight.webp`: an editorial interpretation of Madinah using
  the existing licensed `/marketing/madinah.webp` photograph as a reference.

The exact prompts are recorded in `prompts.md`.
